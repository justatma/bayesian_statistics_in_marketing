# Brief: piece L5, a lead is counted, never valued (D46)

Read first: `spec/decisions.md` rows D33, D38, D41 and D46; `spec/briefs/live_common.md` for the
rules every live builder follows (scope, no network, never read `.env` or any keys file).

## The defect

Jovannah's live run (`data/live/engine/runs/run_5c43f61a.json`, which you may not read: it is
client data; the facts are here) derived `contribution_per_click` for every ad by pricing each
pixel lead as a $795 order from her economics, $126 per click on her biggest ad. D33 and D41: a
lead is counted, never valued. The Meta connector already knows (`meta.is_lead_event`, revenue
None), but `live/refresh.py` hands `pipeline.execute` only rows, so the engine derives every
quantity in `pipeline.DISPLAY`.

## The contract (D46)

1. `core/records.py`: `CompareRequest.conversion_kind: Literal["purchase", "lead"] = "purchase"`.
2. `pipeline.py`: a public constant `LEAD_NOT_VALUED = "a lead is counted, never valued (D33)"`.
   With `request.conversion_kind == "lead"`:
   - a metric outside `RATE_METRICS` raises `NotInScope(PLANNED_OBJECTIVE.format(metric="leads",
     model=PLANNED_TARGETS["leads"]))` before any fit, save or publish (D38.2), next to the other
     early validation in `execute`;
   - `aov`, `roas`, `contribution_per_click` are left out of the derived names; each compared
     variant's `not_estimable` entry names each of them with `LEAD_NOT_VALUED`;
   - the run's `notices` carry `LEAD_NOT_VALUED` exactly once;
   - `cvr` and `cpa` are still derived.
   Purchase runs are byte-for-byte unchanged in behaviour.
3. `live/refresh.py`: `conversion_kind="lead"` when
   `meta.is_lead_event(config.conversion_event.get("meta"))`, else `"purchase"`.
