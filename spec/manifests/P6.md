INFERRED

- PPC p-value is the mid p-value P(T_rep > T_obs) + ½ P(T_rep = T_obs) of the stage total summed over ads (§7.4 says "posterior predictive p-value of the stage total"; mid-p chosen for discrete totals).
- PPC likelihoods mirror the conjugate route: delivery Poisson(spend·1000/CPM), CTR Binomial, CVR Binomial on rounded lag-adjusted trials, value as sum of log-normal orders (orders file) else Gamma(k·orders, AOV/k).
- Tail ESS uses quantiles (0.05, 0.95), the standard definition (Vehtari et al. 2021), held as a code constant rather than a registry threshold.
- A NUTS state's parquet carries a `chain` column and the same `<ad>.<param>` columns; divergences sit in `posterior.params["diagnostics.n_divergences"]`. Without a chain column R-hat and ESS are None and G3 skips them.
- Step 3 "without pooling above the ad level" means `hierarchy=["ad"]`; stage `pooled` flags are left as given. A step whose spec equals the previous one is not refit and emits no notice of its own.
- Every step uses the caller's seed; FitConfig comes from `diagnostics/sampler@v1`.
- Step-4 state is stamped `route.fallback_conjugate = 1.0` in `posterior.params` with a new `state_id` (hash of base id + "fallback"); its report comes from `run_diagnostics` with `passed` unset, since G3 fires after NUTS fits only.
- G3-failed NUTS states are saved to the funnel-state store with `quarantined=True`.
- Step 2 and 3 notice reasons are composed in code ("<step> failed G3 (<G3 reasons>)") because phrasing.yaml has no keys for them.
