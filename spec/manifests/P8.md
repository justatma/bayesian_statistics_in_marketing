- Meta conversions = the default-window `value` of `offsite_conversion.fb_pixel_purchase` in `actions`, revenue the same entry in `action_values`; an absent list or entry counts 0. Per-row `attribution_setting` is the row's attribution window, else the registry default.
- Google Ads REST JSON is lowerCamelCase of the GAQL names, and zero-valued metrics may be absent and read as 0 (F3 §3 INFERRED). `customer.currency_code` and `customer.time_zone` exist (F3 §4 UNKNOWN by fetch). Query A only (no conversion-action segment). Row currency is the customer currency.

- pull_id payload: platform, account, window, attribution windows seen (or the default), `pulled_at`, and a per-process counter so two pulls in one microsecond still differ.
