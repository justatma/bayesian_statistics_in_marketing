## Facts assumed

VERIFIED
- Hold rule: benefit minus switching cost ≤ 0, or decision below floor, gives hold; oracle 1170 − 250 = 920 < 1000 (spec §8.2 C2, `tests/oracle/north_pier.py`).
- Unknown switching cost: stated, `reversibility="costly"`, hold test with zero (§8.2 C1). Cost read from `ChannelModel.learning_phase_switching_cost` via `channels.model.load_channel_model` (§8.2 C1, D22 item 14).
- Survival floor on `Comparison.value_q05` (§8.2 C3, D22 item 7); `allocate` resolves economics with `core.load_economics` (D22 item 9; test file contract assumption).
- `reallocate(..., *, scope=None)` (D22 item 10).
- Stop needs estimable and mature cited cohorts; immature gives `continue_test` with `phrasing/notices/waiting_late`; below floor continues priced against `test_budget`, else `not_knowable` (§8.3 C1 to C3, test file contract assumptions).
- Variance-scaling projection of additional volume, labelled approximate (§6.1 decision basis, NUTS route).
- `min_maturity`, `min_arm_share`, `max_arm_share`, rounding, evidence and route words, the `waiting_late` notice: from registries (I6).
- Decision basis is `decision_loss` (D9); no tolerance gives `not_knowable` with "no loss tolerance set" (D16, reusing `floors.estimability.NO_TOLERANCE_REASON`); I12 for unapproved economics.
- Guarantee prose via `decision.guarantees.render_guarantee` from `guarantee_templates.yaml` only (I8, D15); every Recommendation has `requires_human_approval=True`; params carry no execution keys (I3).
- Audit reads run_record and keys by `OutcomeRecord.source`; ground truth reported apart from AtmaOS (§8.7, test file contract assumption).

INFERRED
- Without a scope, the switching cost is the largest over the platforms listed under `decision_defaults.learning_phase_switching_cost` (meta, google_ads, linkedin), and unknown if any is unknown.
- Without a scope, `Recommendation.scope` is `level="ad"`, `ids={"options": "<comma-joined options>"}`, window = the decision's `checked_at` date (allocate: `checked_at` of its own feasibility), platform None; a notice says scope was not given.
- A known switching cost > 0 makes the switch `costly`; exactly 0 makes it `reversible`.
- Survival floor set but `value_q05` missing for an option: that option is barred (share 0 in allocate).
- Every option, incumbent included, barred by the survival floor: `not_knowable` (reallocate and allocate). Incumbent barred but a candidate eligible and not worth the switch: still `hold`, with a notice (open question 8).
- `stop_or_continue`: a marginal decision is treated like below floor (C3 path), since C1 requires estimable. No `test_budget`, no unit to price from, or a volume that cannot be projected gives `not_knowable`.
- When `Feasibility.required` is None (merged floors never projects it), stopping projects n_total = n_now × (L / tolerance)², n_now the compared options' volume so far in the horizon unit; labelled approximate in the notices and `params["required_projected"]`.
- Remaining cost = volume × observed unit cost of the compared options (spend per click / impression / day; 1 for spend).
- A Comparison option absent from `FunnelData.entities` raises ValueError.
- `allocate`: economics found but unapproved or without a loss tolerance → `not_knowable` with a `none` guarantee naming the reason. Economics not found → the split is still issued with a below-floor feasibility and a notice (open question 5).
- Share bounds: too many options for the floor (n × 0.10 > 1) → floor becomes 0.5 / n; too few for the cap (n × 0.90 < 1) → cap becomes 1 / n. Each disclosed. Shares are `clip(t × p_best, lo, hi)` with t by bisection; zero-p_best arms sit at the floor or share the remainder.
- Guarantee kinds: reallocate/hold/stop_test use `expected_loss`; continue_test, not_knowable and below-floor hold use `none`; allocate uses `posterior_probability`. Braces in slot values are replaced with parentheses.
- Evidence words from `objective.target` (clicks → delivery; orders, contribution → platform_attributed); route words from `Comparison.method` (exact_integration → conjugate, joint_draws → nuts).
- `expected_benefit`: net benefit for reallocate, None otherwise; allocate `expected_loss` is the share-weighted expected loss.
- Audit `realized` keys: `benefit`, `loss`, `regret`, `covered_90` or `interval_90` + `truth`, `not_knowable_correct`, `evidence` (platform_attributed, observed, modeled), `currency`. Metrics live in buckets `<source>.<evidence>`, or `<source>.unclassified` when unlabelled; `by_source[<source>]` holds counts only (n_outcomes, n_recommendations, n_skipped_bad_evidence, n_skipped_currency). `coverage_90` and `regret` keyed by bucket. A bad evidence value, or a currency differing from the recommendation's, is skipped and counted; scored outcomes in more than one currency raise ValueError. `n_recommendations` counts recommendations with a scored outcome. Window: run started on/after `since`, or, with no RunRecord, any outcome recorded on/after `since`.
